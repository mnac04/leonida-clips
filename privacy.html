Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

"Leonida Clips Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Leonida Clips channels to upload short videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

What data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app receives permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel and to read the channel's name. Uploaded content is the owner's own clips.

How the data is used and stored

The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer/server and is used solely to upload videos the owner has approved. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not access data of any other user.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
